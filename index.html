Meet Smt. Kamala Devi
Kanchipuram, Tamil Nadu

"When I loom silk, I do not just interlace threads. I am preserving a rhythm that my grandmother taught me when I was nine years old. KALA gives our craft the modern respect and fair value it truly deserves."

Kamala Devi leads a self-governed guild of 18 master women weavers in Kanchipuram. Under KALA’s co-creation program, her cluster works directly with contemporary designers to adapt traditional motifs into minimalist home throws and unisex stoles.

KALA Brand Evolution Framework

Core Strategic Shift

From a generic transactional marketplace ("Buy cheap Indian handicrafts") to a modern cultural design house ("Invest in living Indian art and slow human craft").

Old Marketplace Model

  • • Price-focused commodity listing
  • • 500+ unverified random products
  • • Anonymous maker profiles
  • • Discount-heavy banner clutter

New KALA Design Model

  • • Editorial value-add story & provenance
  • • Highly curated 10-piece seasonal edits
  • • 70% direct artisan revenue equity
  • • Modern interior lifestyle integration

Why This Solves Craft Pricing Rationale

When buyers see a ₹4,800 Jaipur Blue Pottery vessel on an Amazon-style listing, they compare it with a ₹400 factory ceramic cup. By presenting the 42-hour craft process, artisan guild attribution, quartz frit material study, and minimal styling context, KALA shifts the customer mindset from **price sensitivity** to **value appreciation**.
